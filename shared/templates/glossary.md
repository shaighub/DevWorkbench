## Rules

> A glossary entry is not a requirement. If the definition obliges the product to do something, that obligation is a shall statement in the SRS and the glossary only points at it.
>
> Preferred term only. Record the sponsor's synonym in the definition ("The sponsor also says 'proof of address'.").
>
> Status words used in requirements (`Submitted`, `Active`, and so on) are glossary terms, and their allowed transitions are the functional requirements, not a second copy of the rules buried here.
